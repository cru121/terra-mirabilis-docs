About Terra Mirabilis

Terra Mirabilis is a Civilization VI mod that reworks every Natural Wonder and adds 20 brand-new ones — richer yields, unique ownership effects, and wonders that finally interact with your empire. This is the community 2026 bug-fix update of the original mod.

Install

This is a drop-in mod folder — no Steam Workshop subscription needed.

  1. Download the latest release ZIP (it contains the full mod, including art).
  2. Extract the TerraMirabilis2026 folder into your Civ VI Mods directory:
    Documents\My Games\Sid Meier's Civilization VI\Mods\ (if your Documents are in OneDrive, it's under OneDrive\Documents\…).
    You should end up with …\Mods\TerraMirabilis2026\ containing NaturalWondersMod.modinfo.
  3. Enable it in-game: Additional Content → Terra Mirabilis (2026 update).

Don't run it alongside the original Terra Mirabilis — disable or unsubscribe the original first (this update ships a fresh mod id, so the game would otherwise try to load both). It works with any mix of DLC and expansions; content gates itself to what you own.

The 2026 community update

An unofficial continuation of the abandoned original, fixing long-standing Workshop-reported bugs. It ships as GitHub releases — two so far — and the download button above always grabs the newest.

Latest release (v2) adds one more fix on top of v1:

  • Ubsunur Hollow — earning a Great General now grants its free Inspiration, delivered by the mod's first gameplay script (the original data-only effect could only ever grant a Eureka).

First release (v1) fixed a batch of long-standing bugs:

  • Mount Kailash — culture no longer stacks endlessly on every save/reload.
  • Krakatoa — earning a Great Admiral now actually grants its free Eureka.
  • Matterhorn & Grand Mesa — their movement effects now work and are visible as unit abilities.
  • Victoria Falls — placement loosened so it reliably spawns.
  • Lençóis Maranhenses — yields now apply in the base game, not only Gathering Storm.

How the mod works

Beyond each wonder's own yields and effect, Terra Mirabilis changes some universal rules of the game:

New

Terrain-type interactions

Many wonders visually are a terrain type but are coded as their own feature, so effects that key on the real type used to ignore them. This update teaches them across five classes — e.g. Mountain wonders feed adjacent Terrace Farms, Reef powers an Aquarium, Geothermal wonders enable Thermal Baths, and Marsh / Lake wonders unlock their matching pantheons and wonders.

Wonders power up your districts

Every Natural Wonder gives a standard +1 adjacency bonus to an adjacent Specialty District — Holy Site (Faith), Campus (Science), Theater (Culture), Industrial Zone (Production), and Commercial Hub / Harbor (Gold).

...and their buildings

Buildings inside a Specialty District that sits next to a Natural Wonder gain +1 to the district's base yield, so a wonder-side district keeps scaling.

More wonders on every map

The number of Natural Wonders per map size is roughly doubled (e.g. 10 instead of 5 on a Standard map — placement permitting), and they may spawn a little closer together.

Ownership effects

Most wonders grant a unique ownership effect to whoever controls a tile (shown on each wonder's page), on top of their tile yields.

National Parks pull their weight

National Parks provide Gold equal to their Tourism and extra Amenities to their city.

A livelier settle race

Wonders are more desirable to the AI (higher adjacent fertility) and grant more Era Score and reveal XP, so racing to a wonder matters more.

Nearly all of this is configurable — the mod ships a settings file where each of these can be toggled or tuned, and individual wonders can be turned off. Defaults are described above.
