Mount Vesuvius

Overview

One tile impassable natural wonder. Appears as a Volcano. Adjacent plots yield +1 🔨 Production. Provides additional yields on eruption at the cost of Population and damage to infrastructure. Always active.

Special effect

Antiquity Sites yield +4 🎭 Culture, and 🏺 Artifacts yield +2 🎭 Culture and +2 🧳 Tourism for any Civilization that owns this tile.
